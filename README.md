# BME 2740 MATLAB Companion

**Asad Mirza, PhD**
Florida International University
Department of Biomedical Engineering

The **BME 2740 MATLAB Companion** is an interactive practice resource developed for **Biomedical Engineering Modeling and Simulation**.

The site provides additional opportunities to practice MATLAB, numerical methods, and the concepts covered throughout the course. Students can select a unit, topic, and difficulty level, work through practice questions, and review explanations after submitting their answers.

## What You Can Use It For

* **Practice Questions** — Reinforce material from lectures, assignments, and quizzes.
* **Problem Sets** — Work through multi-part, assignment/quiz-style word problems, with hints and a fully worked solution when you're ready.
* **Review** — Read concise explanations and examples for each course topic.
* **MATLAB Practice** — Write and run MATLAB code directly in the browser.
* **Browse** — Explore questions by unit, topic, difficulty, or keyword.
* **Scratchpad** — Test MATLAB code while working through questions without affecting your quiz results.
* **Progress Tracking** — Keep track of your practice and review progress in your browser.
* **Exam Mode** — Practice with optional time limits to simulate a timed assessment.

## Review Materials

Each major topic has a corresponding Review section containing explanations, examples, MATLAB code, equations, and interactive exercises.

The Review material is intended to complement the lectures and assignments rather than replace them. Use it when you need additional practice or want to revisit a concept before an assignment or quiz.

## Problem Sets

Problem Sets are multi-part word problems in the style of the course's real homework assignments and practice quizzes (parts a, b, c…) — a companion to the shorter practice questions above.

For each problem, you can read the scenario and parts, copy a starter stub into your own script (or open it directly in the MATLAB Sandbox), reveal hints one at a time only if you want them, and then check your work against a fully worked solution with comments.

Problem Sets are ungraded and are not tracked as part of your practice progress.

## MATLAB in Your Browser

The MATLAB Sandbox allows you to experiment with MATLAB code directly in your browser without installing MATLAB.

It is intended as a **practice environment**, not a replacement for MATLAB. Most of the MATLAB functionality used in this course is supported, but some functions and behaviors may differ from the full MATLAB environment.

For graded assignments, always follow the instructions provided for the course and use the required MATLAB environment.

## Your Progress

Practice and review progress is saved locally in your browser. No student identity is associated with this information.

You can also export your progress and import it later if you move to another browser or computer.

## Important Note

This companion is intended to provide **additional practice and review** for BME 2740. It does not replace lectures, assignments, quizzes, or other course materials.

When using any automated feedback or MATLAB execution feature, you should still verify your work and make sure you understand the underlying method. The goal is not simply to obtain an answer, but to develop the ability to formulate, solve, and interpret biomedical engineering models independently.
